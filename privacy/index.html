Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers sanpedrosnorkelingtours.com, a guide to snorkeling Hol Chan Marine Reserve, Shark Ray Alley and the reef off Ambergris Caye, Belize, with a request form for travelers planning a trip.

Snorkel trip requests

When you send a request, we get what you type in the form (for example name, email, dates, group size and notes). It is sent through our publisher Eye To Ad Media's lead server (clients.eyetoad.com) to our inbox. We use it to reply and, when you're asking about a trip, may share it with a San Pedro operator who can take you out. It's never sold.

Measuring visits

Google Analytics 4 places cookies to count visits and record pages viewed, device type, approximate region and the site that referred you. We use the totals, not individual profiles.

Ads from Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

To opt out of personalized ads, go to Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info. More: how Google uses data from partner sites. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent message appears before personalized ads.

Choices and requests

Clear or block cookies in your browser whenever you like. To see or delete a request you sent us, email (appears once the page loads) or phone 1-800-481-8638.

Other

Not directed at children under 13. Updates to this policy are reflected in the date at the top.
